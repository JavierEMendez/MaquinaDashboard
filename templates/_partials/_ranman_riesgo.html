{# RANMAN · Cuadro de Riesgo — faithful port of Valoran's tablero (Sep-2026). Two views (Corte actual / Histórico), the same KPIs, matrices, capacity bars, maturity list, filterable detail table, SVG series charts and notes. Everything is computed client-side from `rd.C` (credits, thousands MXN) and `rd.S` (one record per corte, mdp) exactly as the tablero did, but the facts the tablero hard-coded (bank exits, overdue credits, gaps, lámina-21 reconciliation) are derived from the data so they never go stale. Expects `rd` (ranman_debt) in context. Classes are namespaced `rr-` to stay clear of the dashboard's own .kpi/.grid/.tab styles. #}
